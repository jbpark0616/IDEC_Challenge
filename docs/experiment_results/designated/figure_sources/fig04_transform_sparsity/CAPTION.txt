Figure. Origins of zero rows in the Winograd-transformed activation tiles.
(a) The fraction of all transformed rows that are zero, decomposed into pairs
of zero input rows and cancellation between nonzero input rows.
(b) Zero-row rates for each exact input-row condition; within each pair,
Conv1 is on the left and Conv2 on the right. d_r denotes a four-element input
row vector. Rates use 100 unique input images and accepted channel tiles,
without weighting by output-channel reuse. pp denotes percentage points.
The unsigned 8-bit input transform was verified exactly against RTL outputs
and masks for all 21,900 observed tiles. No datapath changes were made.

Supplementary figure (granularity).
(a) Distribution of transformed tiles by zero-row count.
(b) Element-level zero rates in the corresponding accepted input tiles d and
transformed tiles V. Input pixels shared by overlapping tiles are counted
per tile. This is not the raw-image pixel histogram. Zero-row rates and
zero-element rates have different denominators and do not directly predict
end-to-end cycle savings.
